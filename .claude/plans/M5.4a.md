# Plan: M5.4a References

ADRs: 0094, 0105, 0106, 0107
Base: a74a9da
Program on QEMU: a ring buffer with free functions over `&mut Ring` and
`&Ring`, on riscv32 and thumbv7m at `-O0` and `-O`: `struct Ring { buf: [u8;
8], head: usize, count: usize }` (not `#[copy]`), `push(r: &mut Ring, b: u8)
-> bool`, `pop(r: &mut Ring) -> Option(u8)`, `count(r: &Ring) -> usize` and
`is_full(r: &Ring) -> bool`; `main` fills the ring from a byte string read
through a `&[u8; N]`, drains it through a `&mut Ring` lent to calls in a loop,
reads it through the same `&mut` converted to `&Ring`, and prints the bytes
and the counts over the UART. M5.5 writes the same ring with methods. The
wrong uses (two `&mut` of the ring in one call, a write of the ring while a
reference to it is used later) are ui tests.
Out of scope:
- slices behind references (`&[T]`, `&mut [T]`, `len`, sub-slices), M5.4b
  (roadmap, "M5 steps"); `&raw const`/`*const [T]` slices are unchanged;
- references to `static mut` and `extern static mut`, to places through a
  volatile pointer, to peripherals and clusters: errors here
  (`ty::reference-to-static-mut`, `ty::reference-to-volatile`), M5.4c;
- references returned or stored (ADR 0094), comparing constant indexes (ADR
  0106), `&mut` of a temporary, comparisons of references, LLVM attributes on
  references (ADR 0105): later, each without breaking programs;
- methods, M5.5; moving `core` and the tests to references, M5.7;
- references in the generated programs of ADR 0068, which have only scalar
  types (roadmap, deferred ideas);
- `erebos svd`: it writes no function, so its output does not change.

Before step 1: the `docs(adr)` commit that accepts ADR 0105 to 0107 also sets
the status lines of the ADRs they amend, in their files and in the index
(`docs/adr/README.md`):
- 0008: `Accepted; amended by 0024 (...), 0031 (...), 0105 (`&mut T` converts
  to `&T`)`; index `Accepted, amended by 0024, 0031, 0105`.
- 0095: `Accepted; amended by 0098 (...), 0103 (...), 0104 (...), 0107 (T1 and
  T5 for references; R1 to R3)`; index `Accepted, amended by 0098, 0103,
  0104, 0107`.
- 0097: `Accepted; amended by 0100 (...), 0103 (...), 0105 (no move out of a
  place through a reference)`; index `Accepted, amended by 0100, 0103, 0105`.
- 0098: `Accepted; amended by 0103 (...), 0107 (the kind of a reference; a
  `&mut` argument lent by a reborrow; a borrow is not simple)`; index
  `Accepted, amended by 0103, 0107`.
- 0099: `Accepted; amended by 0100 (...), 0103 (...), 0107 (the place of a
  borrow is a use; `check_body` runs the borrow analysis)`; index `Accepted,
  amended by 0100, 0103, 0107`.
- 0101: `Accepted; amended by 0105 (no linear write through a reference)`;
  index `Accepted, amended by 0105`.
The same commit holds this plan and the roadmap's edits written while
planning (M5.4 split into M5.4a to M5.4c; the M5.4a pending decisions
settled; those of M5.4b and M5.4c listed).

Order: references go through every layer at once (1), since a reference that
type checks must reach MIR, evaluation and code generation, and none of them
can be built without the others' types. The positions a reference may not
take, and what may not be borrowed yet (2), are type errors before MIR gains
R1, which assumes them. The conversion `&mut T` to `&T` and lending at calls
(3) need the MIR borrow of step 1. The analysis (4) reads the borrows and
reborrows of steps 1 and 3. A diagnostic code gets, in the step that adds it,
its snippet in the catalog of ADR 0069 (`tests/rejected.rs`) and its row in
the check tables of `docs/reference.md`; the prose of the reference comes in
the last step. Each step gains a `Decisions:` list while it is built
(`/build-step`), for the choices it makes that this plan and the ADRs do not
dictate.

Between step 1 and step 4, references type check and run without the borrow
check, and between step 1 and step 3 a `&mut` passed twice is a use after a
move; the branch is merged only once step 7 is done.

- Changes:

    place that cannot be written (a `PlaceInfo::immutable` for a place
